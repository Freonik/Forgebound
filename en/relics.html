Relics & sets

Relics only work in their own slot. You can wear one per slot: helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots and off-hand.

How sets work

Every piece of a set is its own legend (its own ritual, one per server). Each piece gives 1/4 of the effect, all four together 5/4. You can mix pieces of different sets, but the full set is clearly the strongest. The action bar shows how many you wear (2/4, 4/4 ✦).

Forgebound is a plugin for Paper 1.21.11. This wiki is generated from the config, so the numbers match the server.
