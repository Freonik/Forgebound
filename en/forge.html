Forge & stars

The forge

Shift + right-click a crafting table with an empty hand. Pick a recipe; the materials show needed / you have. When you have everything, press FORGE.

The ritual

  • The materials are taken and the whole server learns what is being forged – but not by whom.
  • A boss bar shows the time left and a hint where it is. The location is revealed step by step: first only the dimension, at 33 % an area of ±500 blocks, at 66 % ±100 blocks and at 87 % the exact coordinates.
  • The item spins above the table, and the table can't be broken, burned or blown up.
  • At the end lightning strikes and the legend drops on the table. Whoever picks it up owns it.
  • A ritual needs at least 3 players online; if fewer are left, the timer pauses.

One of each

Every weapon and relic can exist only once on the server. As soon as its ritual starts, nobody else can forge it. If the legend is destroyed (lava, fire, the void, despawning), it can be forged again. And if its holder stops playing for a long time, it crumbles and goes back to the forge.

★ Stars

Every legend weapon has 1 to 5 stars (in half steps). Each star above ★★★ adds 7.5 % damage and takes the same off the cooldowns, each one below takes it away. A freshly forged weapon has ★.

  • Dying costs stars: killed by a player you lose 2 ★ on every weapon you carry, any other death costs 0.5 ★. Nothing drops below ★.
  • Stars are earned only at an Ancient Forge.

Ancient Forges

There are three, each stronger: in the Scorched Lands (up to ★★), in the Nether (up to ★★★) and in the End (up to ★★★★★) – it rises out of the void on a floating island when the dragon dies for the first time. A pillar of soul fire rises above each one. Use the anvil holding the weapon: one ritual = one star up. The weapon stays in the forge until the end and then drops there – whoever is standing there takes it. Afterwards the forge cools down for a day.
