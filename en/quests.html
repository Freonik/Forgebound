Quests & economy

📜 The Questmaster

The Questmaster stands at spawn. Every day he has 3 daily quests and every week (from Monday) 1 weekly – the same for everybody. Progress counts anywhere (/quests), the reward is claimed at him. All daily quests in one day give a bonus of 2 tokens. Blocks you placed don't count for mining, and every player counts only once for kill quests.

Bounties

Carrying a legend puts a price on your head: +1 Bounty Token every 10 minutes, up to 20. At 5, 10 and 20 the whole server is told. Whoever kills you claims your whole bounty. The same killer can claim the same player again only after 30 minutes.

In /bounty tokens buy a Legend Tracker (a compass to the nearest legend holder), a random or a chosen legendary material.

🪙 The shop

Bounty Tokens are the server's currency. /shop has a fixed offer and a weekly offer that changes every 7 days. Send tokens with /pay.

Totem of the Forge

A totem of undying that works from anywhere in your inventory. It brings you back at full health, untouchable for 3 s, with absorption, and a burst of fire throws everyone around you back. From big events, some weekly quests and now and then the weekly shop offer.

Legends and /legends

/legends shows every legend on the server (free, being forged, owned) and your own stats with them. If a legend's holder doesn't play for 7 days, it crumbles and goes back to the forge.
