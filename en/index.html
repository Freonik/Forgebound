Forgebound

Legendary weapons that exist only once. Forge them, defend them, don't let anyone take them.

Getting started

  1. Shift + right-click any crafting table to open the forge with every recipe.
  2. Every legend needs one legendary material – from bosses, rare mobs and structure chests.
  3. When you have everything, start the ritual. The whole server learns what is being forged, and bit by bit where.
  4. The finished weapon drops on the table – every legend exists only once, so defend it.
  5. An Ancient Forge gives it stars. Dying takes them away.
