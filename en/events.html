Events

/events shows what's happening right now. Regular events start on their own when a few players are online.

☾ Blood Moon

Some nights the Blood Moon rises. Until dawn monsters have more health and hit harder, come in pairs, Blood-crazed elites appear (3× XP and Bounty Tokens), legendary materials drop more often and nobody can sleep.

✷ Rift

Every 1.5–3 hours a Rift opens near a random player. The coordinates are announced right away and after 3 minutes 5 waves of monsters pour out, up to a Rift Ravager and a Rift Evoker. Rewards are split by how much everyone did; the best player also gets a legendary material.

☠ Legend Hunt

Every 2–4 hours a random legend holder is hunted for an hour: they glow, their area is announced every 5 minutes and their bounty counts double. In return their abilities recharge twice as fast. Survive the hour and they get tokens.

Small events

  • ✦ Happy Hour – double XP, double material drops or rituals twice as fast.
  • ⚖ Forge Market – special offers in /shop.
  • ✉ Golden Shipment – a crate floats down somewhere near spawn; first come, first served.

⚔ Big events

Big events are planned ahead by an admin – the server announces them a day, an hour, 10 minutes and a minute before, and the last 10 minutes count down.

  • ☠ The Closing World – the border closes in on one spot. Dying, logging out or leaving the world puts you out. The last one standing wins.
  • ⚔ The Hunt – everyone shows on the locator bar. The most player kills wins.
  • ☠ The Forge Colossus – a giant boss: it slams, throws fire, drags people in and calls skeletons. Only players can hurt it. The most damage wins.

The winner gets a mythic weapon for 7 days; everyone else tokens, materials and a Totem of the Forge.

✦ Mythic weapons

Far stronger than legends – but they fade after 7 days, wherever they are. No stars, they can't be forged and there can be several.
