The world

🌋 Scorched Lands

A rare burnt biome: ash in the air, a grey-orange sky, slag, blackstone and magma, trees burnt down to stumps. It has ruins – the Ruined Forge with a big chest and guards, the Burnt Workshop, the Slag Heap, the Watchtower – and in one of them an Ancient Forge. The guards are husks in battered armor, and the chests have iron, gold, sometimes diamonds, netherite scrap, Bounty Tokens and (big chests) a legendary material.

The server always has at least two areas 500–1700 blocks from the centre.

☄ Meteors

Every 2–4 hours a meteor falls within 2500 blocks of spawn. A boss bar counts down five minutes and the site is revealed step by step. The crater has a Meteor Cache: always a legendary material, plus diamonds, gold, XP, Bounty Tokens and sometimes a Legend Tracker. Sometimes the Forge Warden guards it.

⚒ The Forge Warden

Sneak + right-click a crafting table holding a Nether Star to wake the Forge Warden – a giant in netherite with 500 HP. It slams, drags people in with chains, calls thralls, breathes fire below 50 % and rages below 25 %. When it falls: 1000 XP, 10 Bounty Tokens and a Warden's Seal for whoever dealt the most damage – right-click it to choose any legendary material.

Forgebound is a plugin for Paper 1.21.11. This wiki is generated from the config, so the numbers match the server.
